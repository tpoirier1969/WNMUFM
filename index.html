What this does: the app flags patterns that could distort a programming or audience conclusion. A flag is a request to review the data, not a declaration that it is bad.

High means the pattern is large enough to materially distort the story the chart tells. Warning means it is unusual enough to investigate but may be legitimate.

Expected closes the flag and keeps the data. Exclude closes the flag and removes that flagged date from daily trend/headline analysis when it can be isolated without rewriting the NPR source. Resolve closes the flag after review but leaves the data unchanged.
